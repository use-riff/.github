# Code of conduct

riff is built around the idea that music communities and artists should be
treated fairly. We expect the same in every riff space: GitHub repositories,
issues, discussions and official social channels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give constructive, specific feedback.
- Respect artists' names, work and consent. Being selected on riff does not
  mean an artist is affiliated with riff or endorses a coin, so don't present
  it that way.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Posting someone else's private information without their consent.
- Scams, impersonating riff, its team or an artist, or sharing fake contract
  addresses or links.
- Shilling, price speculation or pressure to buy any token in project spaces.
- Spam, or repeatedly derailing a discussion.
