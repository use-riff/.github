**riff** is a community-coin launch protocol for music, being built on Solana.

When someone launches a coin on riff, they pick a music artist to support. Part
of the platform fees is set aside for that artist, and the artist can verify
who they are and claim it. If the artist says no, or doesn't claim in time, the
fees go to an independent music charity.

> riff is in research and development. It is **not live yet**, and features,
> architecture and timelines may change. The official channels for
> announcements are the ones linked above.

### How it works

| step | what happens |
| --- | --- |
| **01 · select** | A coin creator picks an artist they want to support. |
| **02 · reserve** | A fixed share of platform fees is set aside for that artist. |
| **03 · claim** | The artist verifies their identity and decides whether to accept. |
| **04 · redirect** | Declined or expired allocations go to an independent music charity. |

Being selected is not the same as being affiliated. Until an artist accepts,
they have not approved or endorsed the coin.

### Design principles

- fixed supply
- transparent allocations
- locked liquidity
- artist consent
- public fee routing
- public launch information

riff is meant to cut down on common creator-controlled rug pulls. It cannot
remove market risk, coordinated selling or loss of value.

### Roadmap

| # | milestone | status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | **Prototype**: first working version on Solana, with coin creation, fee sharing and artist claims | 🔨 building |
| 02 | **Fair launches**: rug-resistant launch standard with transparent allocations, locked liquidity and creator protections | next |
| 03 | **Public testnet**: community testing, product improvements and security reviews | later |
| 04 | **Mainnet beta**: limited public beta on Solana with the first community coins and artist beneficiaries | later |
| 05 | **Robinhood Chain**: build and launch riff on Robinhood Chain after Solana | later |
| 06 | **Expansion**: more artist tools, charity partnerships and more networks | later |
